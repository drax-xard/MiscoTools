Authoring quick guide
  1. Narrow the scope: one language, period, and dialect.
  2. Collect grammars and onomastic sources before inventing roots.
  3. Fill the inventory with IPA and articulatory features you can source.
  4. Map your transliteration to IPA, then add roots and templates.
  5. Add join rules only for real boundary processes.
  6. Record sources, license, and scope notes before sharing.
